 ## 灵修默想 / REFLECTION

    马太福音 22:1-14 / Matthew 22:1-14 —— Kezha 牧师 / Fr. Kezha

在马太福音 22:1-14 中，耶稣讲了一个国王邀请客人参加婚宴的比喻。邀请是慷慨的，但许多人因为忙于自己的事务而没有回应。我们有时也会因为生活的责任和担忧而过于忙碌，以至于忘记给神留出空间。

In Matthew 22:1–14, Jesus tells the parable of a king who invites guests to a wedding banquet. The invitation is generous, yet many are too occupied with their own affairs to respond. We too can become so busy with life's responsibilities and worries that we forget to make room for God.

在腓立比书 4:6-7 中，保罗鼓励我们：“应当毫无忧虑，只要凡事通过祷告和祈求，带着感谢，把你们所要的告诉神。” 保罗是在狱中写下这些话的，提醒我们即使在不确定中，神的平安也是可能的。祷告并不总是改变我们的处境，但它能让我们更接近那位陪伴我们度过处境的主。

In Philippians 4:6–7, Paul encourages us, “Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.” Paul wrote these words while in prison, reminding us that God's peace is possible even amid uncertainty. Prayer does not always change our circumstances, but it draws us closer to the One who walks with us through them.

本周，每当忧虑升起时，让我们停下来，将我们的挂虑带给神，为他的信实献上感谢，并信靠他在基督耶稣里保守我们的心怀意念。

This week, whenever worry arises, let us pause, bring our concerns to God, give thanks for his faithfulness, and trust him to guard our hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.

## 为儿童祷告 / PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN

我们心中牵挂着我们教会及更广泛社区中的儿童和青少年，他们是按您的形象和样式创造的，为您所知，为您所爱。

无论我们的资源丰富还是匮乏，我们都承诺更深入地服务他们。

愿我们的教会成为他们安全的港湾，体验您的生命、旨意与爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见您，愿我们在他们身上遇见您。愿这改变我们双方。奉我们的主耶稣基督之名。阿门。

We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you,

whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply.

May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## 祷告步行——默想与邀请 / PRAYER WALK – A REFLECTION AND INVITATION

    Kezha 牧师 / Fr. Kezha

在过去的八周里，我和 Godfred（Mark See 也多次加入我们）每周都在我们的堂区周围进行祷告步行（Prayer Walk）。我们相信这是圣大卫教堂增长与宣教使命的重要组成部分。

For the past eight weeks, Godfred and I, with Mark See joining us on several occasions, have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. We believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.

当我们走过莫拉宾（Moorabbin）时，我们为社区中所有生活、工作、学习和往来的人祈祷，包括居民、本地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、托儿中心和工作场所。神将我们安置在这里，不仅是为了关照我们自己，更是要通过“在场的服事（ministry of presence）”成为社区中的光。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for all who live, work, study and travel in our community, including residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres and workplaces. God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community through a ministry of presence.

我热切邀请大家在每周四上午 9:30 的晨祷后加入我们。步行大约需要一小时，如果大家有足够的兴趣，我们还可以安排额外的日子。 即使您无法亲自同行，仍然可以通过为祷告步行代祷来参与。请祈求神在我们前面行，预备人心，打开沟通的大门，帮助我们在社区中建立真挚的友谊。愿这些关系引导人们通过我们的咖啡厅、二手店和社区活动来到教会，最终体验到基督的爱。

I warmly invite others to join us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. The walk takes about an hour, and we can arrange additional days if there is sufficient interest. Even if you are unable to join us, you can still participate by praying for our Prayer Walk. Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversations, and help us build genuine friendships within our community. May these relationships lead people to discover our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop and community activities, and ultimately experience the love of Christ.

无论您身在何处都可以参与：在购物、出行或工作时，请为身边的人献上无声的祷告，信靠圣灵正以我们看不见的方式作工。让我们通过祷告、服事和见证基督的爱，一同寻求神对我们堂区和社区的祝福。

You can also take part wherever you are. While shopping, travelling or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you, trusting the Holy Spirit to work in ways we may not see. Together, let us seek God’s blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve and bear witness to Christ’s love.
