默想 | REFLECTION (马太福音 / Matthew 13:44-58)

您生命中最宝贵的财富是什么？在今天的福音书中，耶稣将天国比作藏在地里的宝贝和一颗重价的珍珠——其价值之大，值得放弃其他一切去获取。祂也提醒我们，并非每个人都能认识到它的价值，就像拿撒勒的人们没有认出停留在他们中间的耶稣一样。基督邀请我们超越平凡，去发现神国度无比丰富的宝藏。愿我们永不失去敬畏之心，而是喜乐地珍视基督胜过一切，因为祂是连死亡也无法夺走的唯一宝藏。

What is the greatest treasure in your life? In today's Gospel, Jesus compares the Kingdom of Heaven to a hidden treasure and a pearl of great price—something so valuable that it is worth giving up everything else to obtain. He also reminds us that not everyone recognises its worth, as the people of Nazareth failed to recognise Jesus in their midst. Christ invites us to look beyond the ordinary and discover the incomparable riches of God's Kingdom. May we never lose our sense of wonder, but joyfully treasure Christ above all else, for he is the one treasure that even death cannot take away.

本周，请花时间反思是什么占据了您心中的首位。求神帮助您重新认识基督，珍视祂胜过一切，并作为祂国度的忠心子民度过每一天。

This week, take time to reflect on what occupies first place in your heart. Ask God to help you recognise Christ afresh, to treasure him above all else, and to live each day as a faithful citizen of his Kingdom.

Help our church to grow in numbers, in spiritual commitment to you,

为我们的孩子祈祷 | PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN

我们在心中怀揣着我们教会及更广泛社区中的儿童和青年，他们是按您的形象和样式所造，为您所知，为您所爱。无论我们的资源是丰富还是匮乏，我们都致力于更深地服事他们。

愿我们的教会成为他们体验您的生命、您的旨意和您的爱的安全之地。愿他们在我们身上遇见您，愿我们在他们身上遇见您。愿这改变我们双方，奉我们的主耶稣基督之名。阿们。

We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you, whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply.

May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen

2. 贫困救助食物筹集——感谢致意 | Food for the Needy – Thank You

衷心感谢在冬季慷慨支持我们“贫困救助食物”项目的每一个人。

A heartfelt thank you to everyone who generously supported our Food for the Needy project during winter.

每周一，您的捐赠物品都会被送到莫拉宾（Moorabbin）的 BayCISS，分发给无家可归者和其他有需要的人。由于寒冷月份的需求显著增加，我们到达时货架常常几乎空空如也。您的慷慨奉献带来了实质性的改变，圣大卫教堂再次成为我们本地社区中可见且充满关怀的存在。感谢您的忠心支持。

Each Monday, your donations have been delivered to BayCISS in Moorabbin, where they are distributed to people experiencing homelessness and others in need. With demand increasing significantly during the colder months, the shelves were often nearly empty when we arrived. Your generosity has made a real difference, and once again St David's has been a visible and caring presence in our local community. Thank you for your faithful support.
