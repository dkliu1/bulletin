## 默想灵修 / REFLECTION

经文：马太福音 22:1-14 — Kezha 牧师 / Matthew 22:1-14 - Fr. Kezha

在马太福音 22:1–14 中，耶稣讲了一个国王邀请宾客参加婚宴的比喻。邀请是慷慨的，但许多人却因为忙于自己的事务而没有回应。我们有时也会因生活的责任和忧虑而过于忙碌，以至于忘记为上帝留出空间。

In Matthew 22:1–14, Jesus tells the parable of a king who invites guests to a wedding banquet. The invitation is generous, yet many are too occupied with their own affairs to respond. We too can become so busy with life's responsibilities and worries that we forget to make room for God.

在腓立比书 4:6–7 中，保罗鼓励我们：“应当毫无忧虑，只要凡事借着祷告、祈求和感谢，把你们所要的告诉神。” 保罗是在狱中写下这些话的，提醒我们即使在不确定中，神所赐的平安也是可能的。祷告并不总是改变我们的处境，但它能让我们更接近那与我们同行的一位。

In Philippians 4:6–7, Paul encourages us, “Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.” Paul wrote these words while in prison, reminding us that God's peace is possible even amid uncertainty. Prayer does not always change our circumstances, but it draws us closer to the One who walks with us through them.

本周，每当忧虑升起时，让我们停下来，将所关心的事带给上帝，为他的信实献上感谢，并信靠他在基督耶稣里保守我们的心怀意念。

This week, whenever worry arises, let us pause, bring our concerns to God, give thanks for his faithfulness, and trust him to guard our hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.

## 为儿童与青少年祷告 / PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN

我们心中牵挂着我们教会及更广泛社区中的儿童和年轻人，他们是按着你的形象和样式所造，为你所知、为你所爱。无论我们的资源丰富还是匮乏，我们都致力于更深地侍奉他们。

愿我们的教会成为他们体验你生命、旨意与慈爱的安全之地。愿他们在我们身上遇见你，愿我们在他们身上遇见你，并愿这改变我们双方。因我们的主耶稣基督之名。阿门。

We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you, whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply. May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## 行进祷告：默想与邀请 / PRAYER WALK – A REFLECTION AND INVITATION

弗·克扎牧师 / Fr. Kezha

在过去的八周里，Godfred 和我（Mark See 也多次加入）每周都在我们的教区周围进行“行进祷告”（Prayer Walk）。我们相信这是圣大卫增长与使命的重要组成部分。

For the past eight weeks, Godfred and I, with Mark See joining us on several occasions, have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. We believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.

当我们走过莫拉宾（Moorabbin）时，我们为社区中生活、工作、学习和往来的所有人祷告。上帝将我们安置在这里，不仅是为了关心自己，更是要通过“在场的事工”（ministry of presence）成为社区中的光。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for all who live, work, study and travel in our community... God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community through a ministry of presence.

我热烈邀请更多人加入我们：时间为每周四上午 9:30 晨祷之后（约耗时一小时）。即使无法同行，您也可以通过祷告参与其中。在日常购物、出行或工作时，也可以为身边的人献上默祷。

I warmly invite others to join us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. Even if you are unable to join us, you can still participate by praying for our Prayer Walk.
