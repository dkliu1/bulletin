REFLECTION — Matthew 18:10–20 — Fr. Kezha
信息分享——马太福音18:10–20——Kezha牧师

Peter asks Jesus a question many of us have wondered: “How often should I forgive?”

彼得问耶稣一个许多人都曾经想过的问题：“我应当饶恕多少次呢？”

Surely there must be a limit. Yet Jesus responds, “Not seven times, but seventy-seven times,” pointing not to a number but to a way of life shaped by God's boundless mercy.

我们一定会想，饶恕总应该有一个限度吧。然而，耶稣回答说：“不是七次，乃是七十七次。”耶稣所指的并不是一个具体的数字，而是一种以上帝无限的怜悯为塑造力量的生活方式。

Jesus then tells the parable of a servant whose enormous debt is completely forgiven by his master. Tragically, that same servant refuses to forgive a much smaller debt owed to him.

接着，耶稣讲了一个比喻：有一个仆人欠主人一笔巨额债务，主人完全赦免了他的债务。令人遗憾的是，这个仆人却拒绝饶恕另一个欠他少得多的债务的人。

The lesson is clear: those who have received God's forgiveness are called to extend that same grace to others.

这个教训非常清楚：那些已经领受上帝赦免的人，也蒙召把同样的恩典延伸给别人。

Forgiveness is not always easy. Some wounds run deep, and healing can take time. Yet when we remember how much God has forgiven us through Christ, our hearts are opened to show mercy.

饶恕并不总是一件容易的事。有些伤害很深，医治也需要时间。然而，当我们记得上帝借着基督赦免了我们何等多的罪时，我们的心就会被打开，从而愿意向别人施怜悯。

This week, may we live as people who have been forgiven much and therefore forgive generously.

愿我们这一周都活出蒙了极大赦免之人的样式，因此也能够慷慨地饶恕别人。

PRAYER WALK — A REFLECTION AND INVITATION
祷告漫步——分享与邀请

Fr. Kezha
Kezha牧师

For the past six weeks, Godfred and I have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. I believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.

过去六周，Godfred 和我每周都在教区周围进行一次“祷告漫步”。我相信，这是 St David’s 教会成长和宣教使命中非常重要的一部分。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for the people who live, work, study, and travel in our community—for residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres, workplaces, and community facilities.

当我们漫步在 Moorabbin 时，我们为生活、工作、学习以及出行于这个社区的人祷告——为居民、当地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、幼儿中心、工作场所和社区设施祷告。

God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community and to participate in His mission through a ministry of presence.

上帝把我们放在这里，不只是为了照顾自己，也是为了成为社区中的光，并通过“同在的事工”参与上帝的使命。

I would love others to join us. If you are able, come and walk with us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am.

我非常希望有更多人加入我们。如果您方便，欢迎每周四上午9:30晨祷结束后与我们一起进行祷告漫步。

It only takes about an hour, and additional days can be arranged if there is interest.

整个活动大约只需要一个小时。如果大家有兴趣，也可以安排其他日期。

If you are unable to join the walk, you can still participate by praying for those who do.

如果您无法参加祷告漫步，仍然可以通过为参加的人祷告来参与其中。

Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversation, and help us build genuine friendships within our community.

请祷告，愿上帝走在我们前面，预备人们的心，为我们打开交流之门，并帮助我们在社区中建立真诚的友谊。

Pray that through these relationships people may come to know our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop, and community activities, and ultimately discover the love of Christ.

求上帝使人们通过这些关系，认识我们的教区，认识我们的咖啡馆、慈善旧货店和社区活动，并最终发现基督的爱。

You can also participate wherever you are. While shopping, travelling, or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you and leave the rest to the Holy Spirit.
