REFLECTION – Matthew 18:10–20 – Fr. Kezha
反思——马太福音 18:10–20——Kezha 牧师

In the Parable of the Workers in the Vineyard, Jesus challenges our natural understanding of fairness and invites us to see the world through the lens of God’s grace.
在“葡萄园工人的比喻”中，耶稣挑战了我们对公平的自然理解，并邀请我们透过上帝恩典的眼光来看这个世界。

The workers who laboured all day were upset when those who worked only one hour received the same wage. Yet the landowner reminds them that he has not been unfair; rather, he has been generous.
那些辛苦工作了一整天的工人看到只工作了一个小时的人也得到同样的工资，感到很不高兴。然而，园主提醒他们，他并没有亏待他们；相反，他是慷慨的。

This parable teaches us that God’s kingdom is built on grace, not merit.
这个比喻告诉我们，上帝的国度建立在恩典之上，而不是建立在功劳之上。

Whether we come to faith early in life or later, whether our service seems great or small, God welcomes us with the same love and generosity. Salvation is God’s gift, not something we earn.
无论我们是在年轻时还是后来才信仰上帝，无论我们所做的服事看起来是伟大还是微小，上帝都以同样的爱和慷慨接纳我们。救恩是上帝所赐的礼物，并不是我们靠自己的努力赚取的。

As followers of Christ, we are called to rejoice in God’s blessings upon others and resist the temptation to compare ourselves with them.
作为基督的跟随者，我们蒙召要为上帝赐给别人的祝福而喜乐，并抵制拿自己与别人比较的诱惑。

May we give thanks for God’s abundant grace and extend that same generosity to those around us.
愿我们为上帝丰丰富富的恩典献上感谢，也把同样的慷慨与恩慈分享给身边的人。

PRAYER WALK – A REFLECTION AND INVITATION
祷告行走——分享与邀请
Fr. Kezha
Kezha 牧师

For the past seven weeks, Godfred and I have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. I believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.
过去七周以来，我和 Godfred 每周都在教区周围进行“祷告行走”。我相信，这是 St David’s 教会成长和使命工作的重要组成部分。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for the people who live, work, study, and travel in our community—for residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres, workplaces, and community facilities.
当我们行走在 Moorabbin 的街道上时，我们为生活、工作、学习和往来于我们社区的人祷告，也为当地居民、商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、幼儿照护中心、工作场所和社区设施祷告。

God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community and to participate in His mission through a ministry of presence.
上帝把我们放在这里，不只是为了照顾自己，也是为了成为社区中的光，并通过亲身参与的事工参与上帝的使命。

I would love others to join us. If you are able, come and walk with us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. It only takes about an hour, and additional days can be arranged if there is interest.
我非常希望有更多人加入我们。如果您方便，欢迎每周四上午9:30晨祷结束后与我们一起行走。整个过程大约只需要一个小时，如果大家有兴趣，也可以安排其他日期。

If you are unable to join the walk, you can still participate by praying for those who do.
如果您无法参加祷告行走，也可以通过为参加祷告行走的人祷告来参与其中。

Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversation, and help us build genuine friendships within our community.
请祈求上帝走在我们前面，预备人们的心，打开交流之门，并帮助我们在社区中建立真诚的友谊。

Pray that through these relationships people may come to know our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop, and community activities, and ultimately discover the love of Christ.
祈求通过这些关系，人们能够通过我们的咖啡馆、慈善旧货店和社区活动认识我们的教会，并最终发现基督的爱。

You can also participate wherever you are. While shopping, travelling, or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you and leave the rest to the Holy Spirit.
无论您身在何处，都可以参与其中。当您购物、旅行或工作时，可以默默地为身边的人祷告，其余的交托给圣灵。

Together, let us seek God's blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve, and bear witness to Christ's love.
让我们一起祷告、服事，并见证基督的爱，共同寻求上帝对我们教会和社区的祝福。

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labour in vain.” (Psalm 127:1)
