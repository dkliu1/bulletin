## 默想：马太福音 14:13-21 | REFLECTION: Matthew 14.13-21

在今天的福音书中，门徒只看到五个饼和两条鱼——这远远不够喂饱饥饿的人群。然而，耶稣看到的却不同。他接纳了所奉献的，祝福了它，擘开它，递给他们，将看似不足的东西转化为对众人的丰富祝福。同一个主今天继续在我们生命中做工。我们常常专注于我们所缺乏的：时间、精力、信心或资源。耶稣只是要求我们将我们拥有的交在他手中。当我们聚集在主的桌前，我们铭记基督仍在接纳、祝福、擘开和赐予。他用他的恩典滋养我们，不仅是为了我们自己，更是为了让我们成为别人的祝福。愿我们离开这里时确信：凡托付给基督的，都能为他的荣耀和服侍我们的邻舍而用。

In today's Gospel, the disciples saw only five loaves and two fish—far too little to feed a hungry crowd. Jesus, however, saw something different. He took what was offered, blessed it, broke it, and gave it, transforming what seemed insufficient into an abundant blessing for many. The same Lord continues to work in our lives today. We often focus on what we lack: time, energy, confidence, or resources. Jesus simply asks us to place what we have into His hands. As we gather around the Lord's Table, we remember that Christ still takes, blesses, breaks, and gives. He nourishes us with His grace, not only for our own sake but so that we may become a blessing to others. May we leave this place trusting that whatever is entrusted to Christ can be used for His glory and the service of our neighbours.

## 为我们的孩子祈祷 | PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN

**我们在心中谨记我们教堂及更广泛社区中的儿童与青年人，他们是按您的形象和样式所造，被您所知，被您所爱。无论我们的资源丰富还是匮乏，我们都承诺更深入地服侍他们。愿我们的教会成为他们体验您的生命、您的旨意和您的爱的安全之地。愿他们在我们身上遇见您，愿我们在他们身上遇见您，并愿这改变我们双方。奉我们的主耶稣基督之名。阿门。**

**We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you, whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply. May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen**
