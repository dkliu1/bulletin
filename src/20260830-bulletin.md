## REFLECTION – Matthew 16: 21–28
## 福音反思——马太福音 16:21–28

In today's Gospel, Jesus begins to reveal the true nature of his mission. Having confessed Jesus as the Messiah, Peter struggles to accept that the Messiah must suffer and die.

在今天的福音经文中，耶稣开始揭示祂使命的真正意义。彼得已经承认耶稣是弥赛亚，却难以接受弥赛亚必须受苦并且死亡这一事实。

Like Peter, we often desire the blessings of faith without the cost of discipleship. Yet Jesus makes it clear that the way of salvation is the way of the cross.

就像彼得一样，我们常常希望得到信仰所带来的祝福，却不愿意付上作门徒的代价。然而，耶稣清楚地告诉我们，救恩的道路就是十字架的道路。

Jesus invites us not merely to believe in him but to follow him: “If any want to become my followers, let them deny themselves and take up their cross and follow me.”

耶稣邀请我们的，不仅仅是相信祂，更是跟随祂：“若有人要跟从我，就当舍己，背起他的十字架来跟从我。”

This call is not about seeking suffering but about placing Christ at the centre of our lives, trusting him above our own desires and ambitions.

这个呼召并不是要我们刻意寻求苦难，而是要我们让基督成为生命的中心，在祂里面信靠祂，把对祂的信靠置于我们自己的愿望和人生抱负之上。

The paradox of the Gospel is that true life is found not in clinging to ourselves but in surrendering ourselves to Christ, who leads us from the cross to resurrection and hope.

福音的奥秘在于：真正的生命并不是紧紧抓住自我，而是将自己交托给基督。祂带领我们从十字架走向复活，也从十字架走向盼望。

PRAYER WALK – A REFLECTION AND INVITATION
祷告漫步——反思与邀请
Rev. Kezha
Kezha 牧师

For the past four weeks, Godfred and I have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. I believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.

过去四周，Godfred 和我每周都会在教区周围进行一次“祷告漫步”（Prayer Walk）。我相信，这是 St David’s 教会成长和宣教使命中非常重要的一部分。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for the people who live, work, study, and travel in our community—for residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres, workplaces, and community facilities.

当我们漫步在 Moorabbin 的街道上时，我们为生活、工作、学习以及往来于我们社区的人们祷告——为当地居民、商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、幼儿园、工作场所和社区设施祷告。

God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community and to participate in His mission through a ministry of presence.

上帝把我们安置在这里，并不仅仅是为了照顾我们自己，而是要我们成为社区中的光，通过“同在的事工”（ministry of presence）参与祂的使命。

诚邀大家参加祷告漫步
