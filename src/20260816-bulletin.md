## 📖 默想：马太福音 15:21-28 | REFLECTION - Matthew 15.21-28

在本篇福音书读经中，我们认识了一位女儿深受苦害的迦南妇人。虽然她对以色列来说是个外人，但她怀着卓越的信心来到耶稣面前，大声呼求：“主啊，可怜我吧！”尽管面对看似沉寂的阻碍与沉默，她仍拒绝放弃。  
*In this Gospel reading, we meet a Canaanite woman whose daughter is suffering greatly. Though she is an outsider to Israel, she approaches Jesus with remarkable faith, crying out, “Lord, have mercy on me.” Despite apparent silence and obstacles, she refuses to give up.*

> **她的恒心、谦卑以及对耶稣的信靠最终得到了祂的赞许：“妇人，你的信心是大的！”这段经文提醒我们，上帝的怜悯向所有凭信心来到祂面前的人开放。这位妇人并不依赖自己的身份地位、成就或配得；她只是单纯地信靠基督的慈悲。**  
> **Her persistence, humility, and trust in Jesus are eventually commended by Him: “Woman, great is your faith!” This passage reminds us that God's mercy is available to all who come to Him in faith. The woman does not rely on her status, achievements, or worthiness; she simply trusts in the compassion of Christ.**

> **她的榜样鼓励我们在祷告中持之以恒，特别是在应许似乎延宕或上帝看似沉默的时候。耶稣听见那些真心寻求祂之人的呼求。愿我们也能像这位妇人一样，怀着谦卑、坚持以及对祂丰盛怜悯与恩典的确信来到主面前。**  
> **Her example encourages us to persevere in prayer, especially when answers seem delayed or God appears silent. Jesus hears the cries of those who seek Him sincerely. May we, like this woman, approach our Lord with humility, persistence, and confidence in His abundant mercy and grace.**

> **宣教的上主，唯有祢赐予教会增长，**

> **赐智慧予我们的行为，赐喜乐予我们的崇拜，赐大能予我们的见证。**  
> **求祢帮助我们的教会在人数上增长，在对祢的灵性委身中成长，并在对本地社区的服务中迈进。**  
> 
> *God of mission, who alone brings growth to your church, send your Holy Spirit to give Vision to our planning, Wisdom to our actions, Joy to our worship, and power to our witness. Help our church to grow in numbers, in spiritual commitment to you, and in service to our local community.*

### 2. 为我们的孩子祷告 | Prayer for Our Children

> **我们在心中怀揣着我们教会及更广泛社区中的儿童和年轻人，他们是按祢的形象和样式所造，为祢所知、为祢所爱。无论我们的资源是丰富还是匮乏，我们都致力于更深地服务他们。**  
> 
> 愿我们的教会成为他们安全的空间，去体验祢的生命、祢的旨意和祢的爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见祢，愿我们在他们身上遇见祢。愿这相遇改变我们双方，奉我们主耶稣基督的名。阿们。  
> 
> *We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you, whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply. May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.*
