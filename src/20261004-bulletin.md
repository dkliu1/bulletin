## 反思——马太福音 6:25-34——Kezha 神父

Today we celebrate St Francis of Assisi, remembered for his deep love of God and his care for all creation. Francis saw the natural world as a gift from God and encouraged people to treat every creature with gratitude and respect.
今天我们庆祝亚西西的圣方济各，纪念他对上帝深厚的爱，以及他对一切受造物的关怀。方济各认为大自然是上帝所赐的礼物，并鼓励人们以感恩和尊重的态度对待每一个受造物。

In today’s Gospel, Jesus invites us to look at the birds of the air and trust in our heavenly Father’s loving care. As we give thanks for our pets and all animals, we are reminded that creation reflects God’s goodness and glory. We are called not only to enjoy God’s gifts but also to care for them responsibly.
在今天的福音中，耶稣邀请我们看看天空中的飞鸟，并信靠天父慈爱的看顾。当我们为自己的宠物和所有动物献上感谢时，也提醒我们：受造界彰显了上帝的良善与荣耀。我们蒙召不仅要享受上帝所赐的礼物，也要负责任地照料和守护它们。

May this celebration inspire us to become faithful stewards of creation and instruments of God’s peace in our community, giving thanks for all creatures great and small.
愿这次庆典激励我们成为忠心的受造界管家，成为上帝和平的器皿，在我们的社区中活出和平，并为一切大小受造物献上感谢。

## 为儿童祷告

We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you, whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply.
我们把教会中的儿童和年轻人，以及更广泛社区中的儿童和年轻人交托给您。他们都是照着您的形象和样式被造，为您所认识、所爱。无论我们的资源充足还是有限，我们都愿意更加深入地服事他们。

May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
愿我们的教会成为他们安全的空间，使他们经历您的生命、您的旨意和您的爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见您，也愿我们在他们身上遇见您。愿这一切改变我们双方。奉我们的主耶稣基督之名。阿们。

## 祷告行走——反思与邀请

Fr. Kezha

For the past Eight weeks, Godfred and I have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk. I believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.
过去八周以来，Godfred 和我每周都在教区周围进行祷告行走。我相信这是 St David’s 教会成长和使命的重要组成部分。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for the people who live, work, study, and travel in our community—for residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres, workplaces, and community facilities. God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community and to participate in His mission through a ministry of presence.
当我们走过 Moorabbin 时，我们为生活、工作、学习和往来于我们社区的人祷告——为居民、当地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、托儿中心、工作场所和社区设施祷告。上帝把我们安置在这里，不只是让我们照顾自己，而是要成为社区中的光，通过“临在的事工”参与祂的使命。

I would love others to join us. If you are able, come and walk with us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. It only takes about an hour, and additional days can be arranged if there is interest.
我很希望有更多人加入我们。如果您方便，请在每周四上午9:30早祷之后与我们一起行走。大约只需一个小时，如有兴趣，也可以安排其他日期。

If you are unable to join the walk, you can still participate by praying for those who do.
如果您无法参加，也可以通过为参加祷告行走的人祷告来参与。

Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversation, and help us build genuine friendships within our community. Pray that through these relationships people may come to know our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop, and community activities, and ultimately discover the love of Christ.
请祷告上帝走在我们前面，预备人的心，为交流打开门，帮助我们在社区中建立真诚的友谊。也请祷告，通过这些关系，人们能够通过我们的咖啡馆、慈善旧货店和社区活动认识我们的教区，并最终发现基督的爱。

You can also participate wherever you are. While shopping, travelling, or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you and leave the rest to the Holy Spirit.
无论您身在何处，也都可以参与。在购物、旅行或工作时，都可以为身边的人默默祷告，其余的交托给圣灵。

Together, let us seek God's blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve, and bear witness to Christ's love.
让我们一同祈求上帝祝福我们的教区和社区，在祷告、服事和见证基督的爱中同行。

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labour in vain.” (Psalm 127:1)
“若不是耶和华建造房屋，建造的人就枉然劳力。”（诗篇127:1）
