## REFLECTION — Matthew 18:10–20 — Fr. Kezha

## 灵修默想——马太福音 18:10–20——Kezha 牧师

In today’s Gospel, Jesus tells the parable of the lost sheep and reminds us that “it is not the will of your Father in heaven that one of these little ones should be lost.” These words reveal the heart of God, who knows each of us by name and never gives up on those who wander.
在今天的福音经文中，耶稣讲述了迷失的羊的比喻，并提醒我们：“你们在天上的父也是这样，不愿意这小子里失丧一个。”这些话彰显了上帝的心意：祂认识我们每一个人的名字，也从不放弃那些迷失、徘徊的人。

The shepherd’s willingness to leave the ninety-nine and search for the one lost sheep shows us the depth of God's love. No one is forgotten, overlooked, or beyond the reach of his grace. God seeks the lost, welcomes the wandering, and rejoices when they are found.
牧羊人愿意撇下九十九只羊，去寻找那一只迷失的羊，这让我们看到上帝的爱是何等深厚。没有一个人会被遗忘、被忽视，也没有一个人远离上帝恩典所能触及的范围。上帝寻找失丧的人，接纳迷途的人，并在他们被寻回时欢喜快乐。

As we celebrate Father’s Day, we give thanks for fathers, grandfathers, and all who reflect something of God’s caring love. May we also be inspired to share the Father’s heart by caring for others, praying for those who have drifted away, and trusting in God’s unfailing mercy.
在庆祝父亲节之际，我们为父亲、祖父以及所有体现上帝慈爱的人献上感谢。愿我们也受到激励，以天父的心肠去关爱他人，为那些渐渐远离的人祷告，并信靠上帝永不止息的怜悯。

## PRAYER WALK — A REFLECTION AND INVITATION

## 祷告行走——默想与邀请

Fr. Kezha

Kezha 牧师

For the past five weeks, Godfred and I have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. I believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.
过去五周，我和 Godfred 每周都在教区周围进行“祷告行走”。我相信，这是 St David’s 教会成长和宣教使命中非常重要的一部分。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for the people who live, work, study, and travel in our community—for residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres, workplaces, and community facilities. God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community and to participate in His mission through a ministry of presence.
当我们走过 Moorabbin 时，我们为生活、工作、学习和往来于我们社区中的人祷告——为居民、当地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、幼儿照护中心、工作场所和社区设施祷告。上帝把我们安置在这里，不只是为了照顾自己，更是要我们成为社区中的光，并通过“同在的事工”参与祂的使命。

I would love others to join us. If you are able, come and walk with us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. It only takes about an hour, and additional days can be arranged if there is interest.
我非常欢迎更多人加入我们。如果您方便，欢迎在每周四上午9:30晨祷结束后，与我们一起进行祷告行走。整个过程大约只需一个小时；如果大家有兴趣，也可以安排其他日期。

If you are unable to join the walk, you can still participate by praying for those who do.
如果您无法参加祷告行走，也可以通过为参与者祷告来一同参与。

Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversation, and help us build genuine friendships within our community. Pray that through these relationships people may come to know our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop, and community activities, and ultimately discover the love of Christ.
请祷告，愿上帝走在我们前面，预备人心，为交谈打开机会，帮助我们在社区中建立真诚的友谊。也求主使用这些关系，使人们通过我们的咖啡馆、旧货店和社区活动认识我们的教会，并最终发现基督的爱。

You can also participate wherever you are. While shopping, travelling, or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you and leave the rest to the Holy Spirit.
无论您身在何处，都可以参与其中。当您购物、旅行或工作时，可以为身边的人默默祷告，其余的交托给圣灵。

Together, let us seek God's blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve, and bear witness to Christ's love.
让我们一同祈求上帝祝福我们的教会和社区，在祷告、服侍和见证基督的爱中共同前行。

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labour in vain.” (Psalm 127:1)
“若不是耶和华建造房屋，建造的人就枉然劳力。”（诗篇127:1）
