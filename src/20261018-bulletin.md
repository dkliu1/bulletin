## 牧师默想——马太福音 22:15–22——Kezha 牧师
Giving to God｜献给上帝
Yesterday’s Fete was a wonderful success, and I want to thank all our parishioners, volunteers, supporters, and friends who worked so hard to make it possible. From the stalls, food, books, plants, and children’s activities to the many hours of preparation behind the scenes, everyone contributed generously. The funds raised will support the ministry and mission of St David’s.
昨天的教会义卖活动取得了圆满成功。我谨向所有为活动辛勤付出的教友、志愿者、支持者和朋友表示感谢。从各个摊位、食品、书籍、植物和儿童活动，到幕后长时间的筹备工作，每个人都慷慨地作出了贡献。活动筹集的资金将用于支持圣大卫教会的事工与使命。
In Matthew 22:15–22, Jesus says, “Give to Caesar what is Caesar’s, and to God what is God’s.” Yesterday reminded us that giving is not only about money, but also about offering our time, gifts, and talents in God’s service.
在马太福音 22:15–22 中，耶稣说：“凯撒的物当归给凯撒；上帝的物当归给上帝。” 昨天的活动提醒我们，奉献不仅仅是金钱上的给予，也包括将我们的时间、恩赐和才能献上，用来服侍上帝。
I was especially encouraged by a woman who, when told that the Bible she had chosen was free, replied, “No, I want to give to God.”
有一位女士让我特别感动。当别人告诉她，她挑选的那本圣经是免费的时，她回答说：“不，我想把钱奉献给上帝。”
May we continue to serve generously, welcome warmly, and share Christ’s love with our community.
愿我们继续慷慨地服侍、热情地接待，并与社区分享基督的爱。
## PARISH PRAYER FOR GROWTH｜为教会增长祷告
God of mission, who alone brings growth to your church,
差遣的上帝，唯有您能使教会成长，
send your Holy Spirit to give Vision to our planning,
求您差遣圣灵，使我们的计划充满异象，
Wisdom to our actions, Joy to our worship, and power to our witness.
使我们的行动充满智慧，使我们的敬拜充满喜乐，使我们的见证满有能力。
Help our church to grow in numbers, in spiritual commitment to you,
求您帮助我们的教会人数增长，使我们在属灵生命中更加委身于您，
and in service to our local community.
并更好地服侍我们当地的社区。
## PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN｜为我们的孩子祷告
We hold in our hearts the children and young people in our church and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you.
我们把教会和更广泛社区中的儿童与青少年放在心上。他们都是照着您的形象和样式所造，为您所认识、所爱的孩子。
Whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply.
无论我们的资源充足还是有限，我们都愿意更加深入地服侍他们。
May our church become for them a safe space to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them.
愿我们的教会成为一个安全的地方，让他们体验您的生命、旨意和爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见您，也愿我们在他们身上遇见您。
And may it transform us both, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
愿这经历改变我们双方。奉我们的主耶稣基督的名祷告，阿们。

## PRAYER WALK — A REFLECTION AND INVITATION
祷告步行——默想与邀请
Fr. Kezha｜Kezha 牧师
For the past nine weeks, Godfred and I, with Mark See joining us on several occasions, have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. We believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.
过去九周以来，Godfred 和我每周都会在教区周边进行祷告步行，Mark See 也曾多次加入我们。我们相信，这是圣大卫教会成长与使命中重要的一部分。
As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for all who live, work, study and travel in our community, including residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres and workplaces. God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community through a ministry of presence.
我们走过 Moorabbin 时，会为所有在社区中生活、工作、学习和出行的人祷告，包括居民、当地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、托儿中心和各个工作场所。上帝把我们安置在这里，不只是为了照顾自己，也要借着亲身参与和陪伴的服侍，成为社区中的光。
I warmly invite others to join us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. The walk takes about an hour, and we can arrange additional days if there is sufficient interest.
我诚挚邀请大家在每周四上午9:30晨祷结束后加入我们。祷告步行约需一小时；如果有足够的人感兴趣，我们也可以安排其他日期。
Even if you are unable to join us, you can still participate by praying for our Prayer Walk. Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversations, and help us build genuine friendships within our community. May these relationships lead people to discover our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop and community activities, and ultimately experience the love of Christ.
即使您无法亲自参加，也可以通过为祷告步行代祷来参与。请祈求上帝走在我们前面，预备人心，开启交流的机会，并帮助我们在社区中建立真诚的友谊。愿这些关系带领人们通过教会咖啡馆、慈善旧货店和社区活动认识我们的教会，并最终体验基督的爱。
You can also take part wherever you are. While shopping, travelling or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you, trusting the Holy Spirit to work in ways we may not see.
无论您身在何处，也都可以参与其中。购物、出行或工作时，都可以默默为身边的人祷告，相信圣灵会以我们未必看得见的方式动工。
Together, let us seek God’s blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve and bear witness to Christ’s love.
让我们一同祷告、服侍，并见证基督的爱，求上帝赐福我们的教区和社区。
“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labour in vain.” (Psalm 127:1)
“若不是耶和华建造房屋，建造的人就枉然劳力。”（诗篇 127:1）
