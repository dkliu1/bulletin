REFLECTION – Matthew 16.13-20 / 灵修反思 – 马太福音 16:13-20

In today's Gospel, Jesus asks His disciples a question that remains as important now as it was two thousand years ago: “Who do you say that I am?” It is a question that cannot be answered by repeating what others believe. Each of us is invited to respond personally to Christ.

在今天的福音书中，耶稣向门徒提出了一个两千年来同样重要的问题：“你们说我是谁？”这是一个不能仅靠重复他人信仰来回答的问题。我们每个人都被邀请对基督作出亲身的回答。

Peter's confession, “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God,” was not the result of human wisdom alone but a revelation from God. In the same way, faith is God's gift to us, opening our hearts to recognise Jesus for who He truly is.

彼得宣告：“你是基督，是永生上帝的儿子。”这不仅仅是人类智慧的结晶，更是来自上帝的启示。同样，信心是上帝赐给我们的礼物，敞开我们的心扉，去认出耶稣真实的身份。

This Gospel challenges us to move beyond simply knowing about Jesus to trusting Him with our lives. As we grow in faith, may our words and actions bear witness to our confession that Jesus is Lord. May we, like Peter, boldly proclaim Christ and faithfully follow Him each day.

本段福音挑战我们，要从单纯“了解耶稣”跨越到“将生命托付给祂”。随着我们信心的增长，愿我们的言行能见证我们对“耶稣是主”的宣告。愿我们像彼得一样，勇敢地传扬基督，并在每一天忠心地跟随祂。

PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN / 为儿童及青少年祷告

We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you, whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply.

我们将教会及更广泛社区中的儿童和青少年怀藏于心，他们是按着你的形象和样式所造，为你所熟知，为你所爱。无论我们的资源丰富还是匮乏，我们都致力于更深地服务他们。

May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen

愿我们的教会成为他们安全的港湾，去体验你的生命、你的旨意和你的爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见你，愿我们在他们身上遇见你，并愿这深深改变我们彼此。谨奉主耶稣基督之名。阿门。

2. Ross and Mark visit to Seymour / 罗斯与马克参访西摩（Seymour）

On Monday, 17 August, Ross Newton and Mark Rushby travelled to Seymour to pay our respects to Australia’s service personnel at the Vietnam Veterans Commemorative Walk. The Walk is a moving tribute to all who served in the Vietnam War. Its landscaped gardens, detailed plaques, and sculptural elements tell the story of the conflict and its impact on those who served. As visitors follow the winding path, they are invited to reflect on the courage, sacrifice, and resilience of the veterans. It is a peaceful place of remembrance and learning, ensuring that their legacy is honoured and preserved for generations to come.

8月17日（星期一），Ross Newton 和 Mark Rushby 前往 Seymour，在越南退伍军人纪念步道向澳大利亚的服役人员致敬。这条步道是对所有参加过越战人员的感人致敬。其景观花园、详细的纪念牌匾和雕塑元素叙述了那段冲突历程及其对服役人员的影响。当游人沿着曲折的小路前行时，大家一同反思退伍军人的勇气、牺牲和坚韧。这是一个宁静的纪念与学习之地，确保他们的遗产得到铭记并世代传承。

PRAYER WALK – A REFLECTION AND INVITATION / 祷告行走 – 反思与邀请

Rev. Kezha / Kezha 牧师

For the past three weeks, Godfred and I have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. I believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.

在过去的三周里，Godfred 和我每周都在我们堂区周围进行一次“祷告行走”（Prayer Walk）。我相信这是圣大卫教会增长与宣教使命的重要组成部分。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for the people who live, work, study, and travel in our community—for residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres, workplaces, and community facilities. God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community and to participate in His mission through a ministry of presence.

当我们穿行于 Moorabbin 时，我们为在社区中生活、工作、学习和往来的人们祷告——包括居民、当地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、托儿中心、工作场所和社区设施。上帝将我们安置在这里，不仅是为了关心自己，更是要在社区中作光，通过“同在的侍奉”（ministry of presence）参与祂的宣教使命。

I would love others to join us. If you are able, come and walk with us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. It only takes about an hour, and additional days can be arranged if there is interest.

我非常希望有更多人加入我们。如果您时间允许，欢迎在每周四上午 9:30 晨祷结束后与我们一同步行祷告。全程仅需一小时左右，如果大家有兴趣，还可以安排其他时间。

If you are unable to join the walk, you can still participate by praying for those who do. Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversation, and help us build genuine friendships within our community. Pray that through these relationships people may come to know our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop, and community activities, and ultimately discover the love of Christ.

如果您无法亲自参加步行，仍可以通过为参与者祷告来加入。请祈求上帝走在我们前面，预备人心，打开沟通的大门，帮助我们在社区中建立真挚的友谊。祈求人们通过这些关系，借由我们的咖啡馆、二手店和社区活动了解我们的堂区，并最终认识基督的爱。

You can also participate wherever you are. While shopping, travelling, or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you and leave the rest to the Holy Spirit.

无论您身在何处，都可以参与其中。在购物、乘车或工作时，为身旁的人献上默祷，将其余的一切交托给圣灵。

Together, let us seek God's blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve, and bear witness to Christ's love.

让我们携起手来，在祷告、侍奉和见证基督之爱的过程中，寻求上帝对我们堂区和社区的祝福。

    “Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labour in vain.” (Psalm 127:1)

    “若不是耶和华建造房屋，建造的人就徒然劳力。”（诗篇 127:1）
