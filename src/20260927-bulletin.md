REFLECTION – Matthew 5:1–9 – Fr. Kezha｜默想——马太福音5:1–9——Kezha牧师

In the Beatitudes, Jesus paints a picture of what life in God's kingdom looks like. Rather than celebrating power, success, or status, Jesus blesses the humble, the merciful, the pure in heart, and the peacemakers. These qualities are not signs of weakness but marks of Christian discipleship.
在“八福”中，耶稣描绘了上帝国度中的生命应当是什么样子。耶稣所祝福的，不是权力、成功或地位，而是谦卑的人、怜悯人的人、清心的人以及使人和睦的人。这些品质并不是软弱的表现，而是基督门徒生命的标志。

On this Police Remembrance Sunday, we are reminded that true peacemaking often involves sacrifice. Whether in public service, caring for a neighbour, supporting a friend in need, or seeking reconciliation in a broken relationship, peacemaking requires courage, compassion, and selflessness. Jesus himself is the perfect example, laying down his life for the sake of others and bringing peace between God and humanity.
在这个警察纪念主日，我们被提醒：真正地缔造和平，往往需要付出和牺牲。无论是在公共服务中、照顾邻居、帮助有需要的朋友，还是努力修复破裂的关系，成为和平使者都需要勇气、怜悯和无私。耶稣自己就是最完美的榜样——祂为了他人的缘故舍弃自己的生命，使上帝与人类之间得着和平。

As followers of Christ, we are called to reflect his character in our daily lives. May we be people who serve generously, support one another faithfully, and seek to be instruments of God's peace in our homes, church, and community.
作为基督的跟随者，我们蒙召要在日常生活中彰显祂的品格。愿我们成为乐意慷慨服事的人，忠心彼此扶持，并努力成为上帝和平的器皿，在我们的家庭、教会和社区中传递上帝的和平。

God of mission, who alone brings growth to your church,
send your Holy Spirit to give Vision to our planning,
Wisdom to our actions, Joy to our worship, and power to our witness.
Help our church to grow in numbers, in spiritual commitment to you,

## PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN｜为我们的儿童祷告

We hold in our hearts the children and young people in our church,
and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you.
Whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply.

我们把教会以及更广泛社区中的儿童和年轻人放在心中。他们都是按照您的形象和样式被造，为您所认识，也为您所爱。无论我们的资源丰富还是有限，我们都愿意更加深入地服事他们。

May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
愿我们的教会成为他们安全的地方，使他们能够经历您的生命、您的旨意和您的爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见您，也愿我们在他们身上遇见您。愿这一切通过我们的主耶稣基督改变我们双方。阿们。

PRAYER WALK – A REFLECTION AND INVITATION｜祷告漫步——分享与邀请

## Fr. Kezha｜Kezha牧师

For the past Eight weeks, Godfred and I have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. I believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.
过去八周以来，我和 Godfred 每周都在我们教区周围进行“祷告漫步”。我相信，这是圣大卫教会成长和开展使命的重要组成部分。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for the people who live, work, study, and travel in our community—for residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres, workplaces, and community facilities. God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community and to participate in His mission through a ministry of presence.
当我们走过 Moorabbin 时，我们为生活、工作、学习以及往来于我们社区中的人祷告——为居民、当地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、儿童托管中心、工作场所和社区设施祷告。上帝把我们安置在这里，不只是为了照顾自己，更是要使我们成为社区中的光，并通过“同在的服事”参与祂的使命。

I would love others to join us. If you are able, come and walk with us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. It only takes about an hour, and additional days can be arranged if there is interest.
我非常希望有更多人加入我们。如果您方便，欢迎在每周四上午9:30晨祷之后，与我们一起进行祷告漫步。整个过程大约只需一个小时；如果大家有兴趣，也可以安排其他日期。

If you are unable to join the walk, you can still participate by praying for those who do. Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversation, and help us build genuine friendships within our community. Pray that through these relationships people may come to know our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop, and community activities, and ultimately discover the love of Christ.
如果您无法参加祷告漫步，也可以通过为参加的人祷告来参与其中。请祷告，愿上帝走在我们前面，预备人的心，打开交流的大门，帮助我们在社区中建立真诚的友谊。愿人们通过这些关系，通过我们的咖啡馆、旧货店以及社区活动认识我们的教区，并最终发现基督的爱。

You can also participate wherever you are. While shopping, travelling, or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you and leave the rest to the Holy Spirit.
您也可以无论身在何处都参与其中。当您购物、旅行或工作时，可以默默地为身边的人祷告，其余的就交托给圣灵。

Together, let us seek God's blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve, and bear witness to Christ's love.
让我们一起祈求上帝祝福我们的教区和社区，在祷告、服事以及见证基督的爱中共同前行。

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labour in vain.” (Psalm 127:1)
“若不是耶和华建造房屋，建造的人就枉然劳力。”（诗篇127:1）
