# 热烈欢迎 | A WARM WELCOME

热烈欢迎今天与我们一起敬拜的每一个人，特别是初次访问或第一次加入我们的朋友。我们非常高兴您的到来。

A warm welcome to everyone worshipping with us today, especially if you are visiting or joining us for the first time. We are delighted that you are here.

如果你习惯在自己的基督徒传统中接受圣餐，我们热切邀请你在主的餐桌前与我们一同分享。仪式结束后，请加入我们的早茶会，届时将有机会结识我们教会大家庭的成员。如果您能在我们的访客簿上签名，我们将不胜感激。

If you are accustomed to receiving Holy Communion in your own Christian tradition, you are warmly invited to share with us at the Lord's Table. Following the service, please join us for morning tea, where there will be an opportunity to meet members of our church family. We would also appreciate it if you could sign our Visitors' Book.

能与您在一起是我们的喜乐，我们祈祷在我们共同敬拜时，您能感受到神的同在与祝福。

It is a joy to have you with us, and we pray that you will know God's presence and blessing as we worship together.
默想 - 马太福音 14:22-36 | REFLECTION - Matthew 14.22-36

深夜里，当门徒们在风浪中艰难挣扎时，耶稣履海来到他们身边。彼得凭着信心迈出步伐，但当他把注意力从耶稣转向暴风雨时，便开始下沉。耶稣立刻伸出手救了他。后来在基尼烈，人们把病人带到耶稣面前，相信只要摸一摸祂衣裳的繸子就能得医治，而他们并没有失望。这两段事迹提醒我们：信心并不是没有恐惧，而是在恐惧之中依然信靠耶稣。无论我们是在与生活的暴风雨搏斗，还是承受着疾病、悲伤或不确定性的重担，基督都邀请我们将目光注视着祂。祂永远近在咫尺，随时准备扶持、医治并紧紧握住那些信靠祂的人。

In the middle of the night, as they struggled against the wind and waves, Jesus came to them walking on the water. Peter stepped out in faith, but when he focused on the storm instead of Jesus, he began to sink. Immediately, Jesus reached out His hand and saved him. Later, in Gennesaret, people brought the sick to Jesus, believing that even touching the edge of His cloak would bring healing, and they were not disappointed. These two stories remind us that faith is not the absence of fear but trusting Jesus in the midst of it. Whether we are battling life's storms or carrying burdens of illness, grief, or uncertainty, Christ invites us to keep our eyes on Him. He is always near, ready to strengthen, restore, and hold fast those who place their trust in Him.
教区复兴祈祷文 | PARISH PRAYER FOR GROWTH

赐予使命的神啊，唯有您能使您的教会增长。求您差遣圣灵，为我们的规划赐下异象，为我们的行动赐下智慧，为我们的敬拜赐下喜乐，为我们的见证赐下能力。求您帮助我们的表现在人数上增长，在对您的灵性委身中增长，并在对我们当地社区的服务中增长。

God of mission, who alone brings growth to your church, send your Holy Spirit to give Vision to our planning, Wisdom to our actions, Joy to our worship, and power to our witness. Help our church to grow in numbers, in spiritual commitment to you, And in service to our local community.
为我们的孩子祈祷 | PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN

我们在心中记挂着我们教会及更广泛社区中的儿童和青少年，他们是照着您的形象和样式所造，被您所知，也被您所爱。无论我们的资源丰富还是匮乏，我们都致力于更深地服务他们。愿我们的教会成为他们安全的港湾，去体验您的生命、您的旨意和您的爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见您，愿我们也在他们身上遇见您。愿这一切改变我们双方。谨奉我们的主耶稣基督之名。阿们。

We hold in our hearts, the children and young people in our church, and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you, whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply. May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both, Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen
