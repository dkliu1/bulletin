## 二、灵修分享——马太福音 22:34–46
REFLECTION — Matthew 22:34–46
分享者：Kezha 牧师（Fr. Kezha）

在今天的福音中，耶稣用两条诫命概括了上帝律法的核心：要尽心、尽性、尽意爱主你的上帝；并要爱人如己。

In today’s Gospel, Jesus brings the whole of God’s law into focus through two commandments: love God with all your heart, soul and mind, and love your neighbour as yourself.

这两条诫命是密不可分的。我们对上帝的爱，应当体现在我们如何对待他人；而我们对邻人的爱，则源自我们与上帝的关系。

These two commandments belong together. Our love for God must be reflected in how we treat others, while our love for our neighbour flows from our relationship with God.

耶稣教导我们，全部律法和先知的教训都系于这两条诫命。因此，信仰不仅仅是知道上帝的诫命，更要通过怜悯、善良、宽恕和慷慨将诫命实践出来。

Jesus teaches that all the Law and the Prophets hang on these commandments. Faith is therefore more than knowing God’s commandments; it is living them out through compassion, kindness, forgiveness and generosity.

耶稣也挑战法利赛人思考弥赛亚究竟是谁。他的问题邀请我们认识到，耶稣不仅是一位教师，更是我们的主。

Jesus also challenges the Pharisees to consider who the Messiah truly is. His question invites us to recognise Jesus not merely as a teacher, but as Lord.

本周，我们可以怎样通过爱邻人来表达对上帝的爱？

How can we show our love for God by loving our neighbours this week?

## 四、为我们的孩子祷告
PRAYER FOR OUR CHILDREN
我们把教会以及更广泛社区中的儿童和年轻人放在心中。他们都是照着祢的形象和样式所造，为祢所认识、所爱。无论我们的资源充足还是有限，我们都愿意更加深入地服侍他们。

We hold in our hearts the children and young people in our church and broader community, made in your image and likeness, known to you and loved by you. Whether our resources are plentiful or scarce, we commit to serving them more deeply.

愿我们的教会成为他们安全的港湾，使他们能够体验祢的生命、祢的旨意和祢的爱。愿他们在我们身上遇见祢，也愿我们在他们身上遇见祢。愿这相遇使我们双方都得到改变。

May our church become for them a safe space, to experience your life, your purpose and your love. May they encounter you in us. May we encounter you in them. And may it transform us both.

以上祷告是奉我们的主耶稣基督的名。阿们。

Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## 九、祷告行走——分享与邀请
PRAYER WALK — A REFLECTION AND INVITATION
Kezha 牧师 / Fr. Kezha

过去十周以来，我和 Godfred 每周都会在堂区周边进行祷告行走，Mark See 也曾多次加入我们。我们相信，这是圣大卫教会成长与履行使命的重要部分。

For the past ten weeks, Godfred and I, with Mark See joining us on several occasions, have been undertaking a weekly Prayer Walk around our parish. We believe this is an important part of the growth and mission of St David’s.

当我们走过 Moorabbin 时，会为社区中所有生活、工作、学习和出行的人祷告，包括居民、当地商家、超市、咖啡馆、火车站、学校、托儿中心和各类工作场所。上帝把我们放在这里，不只是为了照顾自己，也是要我们通过亲身陪伴和关怀，成为社区中的光。

As we walk through Moorabbin, we pray for all who live, work, study and travel in our community, including residents, local businesses, supermarkets, cafés, train stations, schools, childcare centres and workplaces. God has placed us here not simply to care for ourselves, but to be a light in our community through a ministry of presence.

我诚挚邀请大家在每周四上午祷告早课后（上午9:30）与我们一起参加祷告行走。步行大约需要一小时。如果有足够的人感兴趣，我们也可以安排其他日期。

I warmly invite others to join us on Thursdays following Morning Prayer at 9:30 am. The walk takes about an hour, and we can arrange additional days if there is sufficient interest.

即使您无法亲自参加，也可以通过为祷告行走祷告来参与。请祈求上帝走在我们前面，预备人们的心，开启交流的机会，并帮助我们在社区中建立真诚的友谊。愿这些关系引导人们通过我们的咖啡馆、慈善二手商店和社区活动认识本堂区，并最终体验基督的爱。

Even if you are unable to join us, you can still participate by praying for our Prayer Walk. Please pray that God will go before us, prepare hearts, open doors for conversations, and help us build genuine friendships within our community. May these relationships lead people to discover our parish through our Café, Thrift Shop and community activities, and ultimately experience the love of Christ.

无论您身在何处，也都可以参与。在购物、出行或工作时，可以默默地为身边的人祷告，相信圣灵会以我们未必看得见的方式动工。

You can also take part wherever you are. While shopping, travelling or at work, offer a silent prayer for those around you, trusting the Holy Spirit to work in ways we may not see.

让我们同心寻求上帝对堂区和社区的祝福，在祷告、服侍中一同见证基督的爱。

Together, let us seek God’s blessing on our parish and community as we pray, serve and bear witness to Christ’s love.
